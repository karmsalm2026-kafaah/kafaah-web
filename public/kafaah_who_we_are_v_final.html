WHO WE ARE

An independent consultancy built
inside real industrial plants.

Kafaah specializes in inorganic chemical and fertilizer plant commissioning, startup, troubleshooting, and performance optimization. We don't consult from a distance — we have managed startups, resolved production issues, optimized yields, and trained the operators who run these facilities every day.

We bring nearly 20 years of direct operational expertise across H₂SO₄, H₃PO₄, K₂SO₄, NPK, MgSO₄, and SSP plants — not as observers, but as engineers who have operated them.

The word Kafaah (كفاءة) means competence in Arabic. It is not a marketing name — it is our standard. Every engagement we accept must meet it.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Four things that set Kafaah apart
from every other consultancy.

Independent by Structure

We serve both project owners and EPC contractors — but never on opposite sides of the same project. When we take an engagement, our full accountability is to one client only.

Built Around Operations

Our expertise comes from inside operating plants, not from generic consulting frameworks. When we troubleshoot, we draw on real experience — not theoretical knowledge.

Chemical-Specific Depth

We specialize exclusively in inorganic acids and specialty fertilizers. We do not try to cover every industry — because depth of expertise matters more than breadth of portfolio.

Results Documented

We measure before and after. Our recommendations are backed by data, and our results are documented — because "measured before and after" means more than any claim we could make.

HOW WE WORK

One project. One client. No conflicts.

Kafaah works alongside both project owners and EPC contractors — bringing independent technical expertise to whichever side we represent. Each engagement has one client, one scope, and full accountability.

We never represent both sides on the same project simultaneously. When we work for an owner, we work for the owner alone. When we support an EPC contractor, our accountability is to them alone.

This isn't just a policy — it's the foundation of the independent advice you're paying for.
